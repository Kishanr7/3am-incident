# Private pilot: case #1

**Goal:** learn whether data engineers who did not build it can solve it, enjoy it, and want more. In particular, whether they would play a **short daily version**.

## Who (5–8 people)

- Work with SQL and data pipelines weekly (data engineers, analytics engineers, senior analysts).
- At least 2 outside your current team, ideally 2 outside India.
- Not: people who saw it being built, or non-SQL folks (save them for the "easy daily" test later).

## How to send it

Send `index.html` (zip it, or use a Drive/OneDrive link if email blocks `.html`). Write the message in your own words; here's a starting point:

> Hey, I built a small browser game for data folks: the CFO's revenue dashboard is wrong at 3 a.m., every job is green, and you find out why with SQL. It takes 10–25 minutes. Would you play it once, **without asking me for help**, and send me your result line plus 2 minutes of feedback? It runs fully in your browser on fake data. Honest feedback is the whole point, including "meh".

Don't coach. If they ask for help, say "use a hint" and write down where they got stuck.

## Feedback form (copy into a message or a Google Form)

1. Your result line (the 🕒 text from the end screen):
2. Did you finish? If not, where did you stop?
3. Where did you get stuck or confused?
4. How did it feel: too easy / about right / too hard?
5. Was it fun? (1–5)
6. Would you play a **new 5–10 minute case every day**, like Wordle or the LinkedIn games? (no / maybe / yes)
7. Would you send it to someone? Who? (a teammate, your team channel, nobody)
8. Anything that felt unrealistic to you as a data person?
9. One thing you'd change:

## Thresholds (decided before results arrive, and not lowered afterwards)

**Continue to a daily prototype if all are true:**
- ≥ 4 of 5 finish without help from you (hints are fine)
- Typical solve time is 10–25 minutes
- Fun ≥ 4 for at least 3 people
- At least 3 answer "yes" to question 6 **or** name someone they'd send it to, unprompted

**Rethink if:** most finish but fun is ≤ 3 (the puzzle works, the game doesn't).

**Stop if:** fewer than half finish, or nobody would play again.

Stars, compliments and "cool idea" do not count. Finishing, wanting another case and forwarding it do.
